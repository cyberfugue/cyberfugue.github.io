Aeonfall · iPhone and iPad

Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

In short: the app collects no data at all. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no purchases and no tracking, and nothing you do in it is ever sent to us or anyone else.

What stays on your device

So that you can carry on where you left off, the app saves your chronicle on your phone or tablet: an automatic save, three save slots you fill yourself from the menu, and whether you have the sound on. This is kept in the app's own storage. It isn't sent anywhere and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

You can start a new chronicle at any time from the title screen; a save slot is replaced whenever you save over it.

Network

Everything the game needs, from every world to every sound, is inside the app, so it plays without a connection. The app makes no network requests of any kind and contains no links out.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. If the app costs something to download, that purchase is made through the App Store and handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; we never receive your name, email, payment details or account.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

The website

This page, the support page and the app's page (cyberfugue.github.io) are hosted by GitHub Pages. They load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google, and GitHub, receive the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. The pages themselves set no cookies and run no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
