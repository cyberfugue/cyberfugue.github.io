Aeonfall: help

Questions, problems or a chronicle that won't load: write to cyberfugue@gmail.com.

Say which device you're using, which version of the game (it's on the title screen and under ☰ › About & privacy) and what happened, and you'll get a reply as soon as possible.

Getting started

Drag on the left half of the screen and a joystick appears under your thumb; or tap anywhere you want to walk. Tap a person to talk, a foe to fight, and a chest, bed, sign, rift or ship's helm to use it. A long press looks at something. The ⚔ button strikes the nearest foe.

Lost? Open the 📜 journal: for every quest it says what to do next. The ☰ menu has a How to play page.

Saving

The game saves itself every few seconds, whenever you go somewhere new, and whenever you leave the app. Choose Continue on the title screen to carry on. The ☰ menu also has three save slots for keeping more than one chronicle. Saves live on your device only and don't sync between devices; deleting the app deletes them.

The Hourwell Astrolabe

Turning the hours costs chronosand: tap a glowing rift-seam to gather some; each refills once a day. You need open sky above you, not a dungeon roof. On Caerwyn you arrive exactly where you stood. Each of Earth's past ages opens only once its anchor is set into the dial (find the anchors in the year 2241, then use them from your pack).

The Tether Bracer

Plant a beacon anywhere, then recall to it later, but only within the same star system and the same age. Each recall uses a charge; sleep in a bed, or tap a console, to recharge. The Bracer can also bring you back aboard the Lark.

The Wandering Lark

Step onto the landing pad where she stands to board, and tap the helm at her bow. The star map flies to any world or station under the same sun. The fold chart jumps to other stars once she has a fold core, burning aethercite: charted stars from any distance, uncharted ones within the ring on the chart. Walk down the ramp aft to go ashore.

Companions

Ask a companion to join from their conversation; up to three travel with you. Talk with them each day (How fare you?) and go on quests together, and their stories unfold. Some may come to love you; the one you court must be the only one.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 15 or later. The game plays in portrait or landscape and needs no connection.

Privacy

The game collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The full privacy policy has the details.
