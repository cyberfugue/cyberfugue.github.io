The Oz Books · iPhone and iPad

The Oz Books: help

Questions, problems or a purchase that didn’t go through: write to cyberfugue@gmail.com.

Say which device you’re using, which book you were reading and what happened, and you’ll get a reply as soon as possible.

What’s free, and what the purchases add

The Wonderful Wizard of Oz is free and complete, with all of W. W. Denslow’s pictures, its journey on the map, its people and its reading guide. Every other book shows its chapters, journey, people and reading guide before you buy it.

Baum’s Oz: the thirteen Oz books L. Frank Baum wrote after the Wizard and his six Little Wizard Stories, with John R. Neill’s pictures.

The Royal Historians: fifteen Oz books by Ruth Plumly Thompson and Jack Snow’s two, with the pictures of John R. Neill and Frank Kramer. It is offered only in countries where these books are in the public domain.

The Complete Oz: both collections, and every Oz book added to the app later.

Each is bought once. There is no subscription and there are no ads. With Family Sharing, the people in your family group can use them too.

Restoring your purchases

On a new iPhone or iPad, or after reinstalling, open the app and tap Restore purchases. It’s on the page of every book you haven’t opened yet, under Get the Books in the library, and in the Aa menu. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. You won’t be charged again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Reading

Open a book from the library and pick a chapter, or tap Continue to go back to where you stopped. The Aa menu sets the text size, Day or Night, and whether the people are marked in the text. Where a book has more than one illustrated edition, such as The Marvelous Land of Oz, choose its pictures on the book’s page or in the Aa menu.

Tap any picture to see it full size. Pictures in the top bar opens the gallery of the book you are reading.

The map and the people

On the map, choose a book, then pick a stop to see what happens there; Read this part opens the book at that page. The stops you have read past turn gold. In the text, the first mention of each person in a chapter is underlined: tap it for a short note about them.

Highlights, notes and bookmarks

Select words to highlight them, add a note or bookmark the paragraph. The bookmark button in the top bar marks the place you are reading. The Notes page lists them all, and Export saves a copy you can keep or Import on another device. They don’t sync between devices on their own.

Your place

The app remembers your place in every book on that device.

Privacy

The app collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your place, notes and settings stay on your device. The full privacy policy has the details.
