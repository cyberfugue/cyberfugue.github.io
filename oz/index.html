The books as they were first printed

The Oz books were among the most lavish children’s books of their day: colour plates, drawings printed in coloured inks, pictorial initials and lettered chapter headings. This edition keeps all 3,552 of those pictures, each beside the passage it shows, with the complete text of every book.

“There is no place like home.”
I

Thirty-two books

Baum’s fourteen Oz books and his Little Wizard Stories, the fifteen Ruth Plumly Thompson wrote after him as the new Royal Historian of Oz, and Jack Snow’s two. Each in the order it was published, with its contents, its pictures and its own map.

II

Read with the pictures

The chapter headings, drawings and colour plates of W. W. Denslow, John R. Neill and Frank Kramer sit where the books printed them. Choose your text size and Day or Night, and the app keeps your place in every book.

III

Every picture, full size

Tap any picture to see it whole, with a description and a link to its passage. Each book has a gallery of its colour plates, chapter headings and small drawings. The Marvelous Land of Oz comes in two editions, and the colour plates of Tik-Tok of Oz and The Magic of Oz are restored from first-edition copies.

IV

A map of every journey

Oz and the countries around it, with the road each book travels: 309 stops across the Munchkin, Winkie, Gillikin and Quadling Countries, the lands beyond the desert and under the earth. Pick a stop to see what happens there and open the book at that page. The stops you have read past turn gold.

V

The people of Oz

182 people, from Dorothy and Toto to the King of the Fairy Beavers. The first mention of each in a chapter is underlined: tap it for a short note and their portrait. Each book has a reading guide too, for families, classes and book groups.

VI

Your own marks

Select words to highlight them, add a note or bookmark the place. Search one book or all of them. Everything is inside the app and reads without a connection.

About this edition

The text and pictures come from the illustrated first editions, as transcribed by volunteers, with colour plates for Tik-Tok of Oz and The Magic of Oz from copies scanned by the Internet Archive. The map of Oz, the notes on the people, the descriptions of the pictures and the reading guides were made for this edition.

The app collects no data. It works on iPhone and iPad.
