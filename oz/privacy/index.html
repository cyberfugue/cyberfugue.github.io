The Oz Books · iPhone and iPad

Privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026

In short: the app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you do in it is sent to us.

What stays on your device

To let you carry on where you left off, the app saves a few things on your iPhone or iPad: your place in each book, the stops on the map you have read past, your highlights, notes and bookmarks, your settings (text size, Day or Night, which pictures to read with, whether people are marked), and which of the purchases you own. This stays on your device. It isn’t sent anywhere and we can’t see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Export on the Notes page saves a copy of your highlights, notes and bookmarks as a file, only when you ask it to, wherever you choose to keep it.

Purchases

The in-app purchases (Baum’s Oz, The Royal Historians and The Complete Oz) are made and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment and account details under its own privacy policy. The app only asks the App Store which purchases are on sale where you are, at what price, and which of them your Apple Account owns, so it can open those books. We never receive your name, email, payment details or Apple Account.

Network

The books, their pictures and the fonts are inside the app, so it reads without a connection. Apart from the App Store requests above, the app makes no network requests.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

The website

This page, the support page and the app’s page (cyberfugue.github.io) are hosted by GitHub Pages. They load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google, and GitHub, receive the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. The pages themselves set no cookies and run no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
