The Pilgrim’s Progress: help

Questions or problems: write to cyberfugue@gmail.com.

Say which device you're using and what happened, and you'll get a reply as soon as possible.

Free, all of it

The app is free, and so is everything in it: both parts of the book, the narration, the theatre, the map, the Scrip and Choose the Way. There are no purchases, no subscription and no ads.

The narration

Both parts are read aloud in two LibriVox recordings, by Joy Chan and by MaryAnn Spiegel. Both are stored inside the app, so they play without a connection, and they keep playing with the screen locked. While a voice reads, the text follows it; tap any paragraph to hear it from there. Choose the voice, and how loud it and the sound of the Way are, in Settings.

Your place in the book

The app remembers the stage and place you reached, and your settings, on that device. It doesn't sync between devices. To start afresh, tap Reset progress under Your progress in Settings; to keep nothing between visits, turn off Keep my place on this device there. Your settings stay.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 15 or later. On iPhone Duo the app resizes as you fold and unfold it.

Privacy

The app collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The full privacy policy has the details.

About the app

The text of both parts, Barnard's engravings and the LibriVox recordings are in the public domain. The theatre, sound, map and Choose the Way were made for this edition.
