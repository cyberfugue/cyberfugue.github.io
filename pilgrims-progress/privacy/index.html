The Pilgrim’s Progress

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

The app collects nothing

The Pilgrim’s Progress for iPhone and iPad collects no personal data and shares none. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that does.

What stays on your device

Your place in the book, the stages you have read, your settings (language, text, voice, sound, text size) and your path through Choose the Way are kept on your device, so the app can open where you left off. They never leave it, and deleting the app deletes them.

You choose whether it is kept. Under Your progress in Settings, turn off Keep my place on this device and the app keeps nothing of your progress, forgetting what it had; Reset progress forgets it at once. Either way your settings stay as they are.

No network requests

The book, the engravings, the fonts and all the narration are inside the app, so it makes no requests of its own over the Internet. If you tap a link to another site (LibriVox, or a Bible passage in Portuguese), it opens in your browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

The website

This page, the support page and the app’s page (cyberfugue.github.io) are hosted by GitHub Pages. They load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google, and GitHub, receive the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. The pages themselves set no cookies and run no analytics.

Children

Since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.
