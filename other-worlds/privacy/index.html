Other Worlds · iPhone, iPad and Android

Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

In short: the app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone.

What stays on your device

So that you can carry on where you left off, each of the six books keeps a few things on your phone or tablet: your place in it, how far you have read, what you have seen and done in its own places, and your settings for it (text size, sound and music levels, who reads, and its reading aids). The shelf keeps which book you last opened and what the store said you own, so it can show it at once. Each book’s reading, once downloaded, is kept on the device too, until you remove it; on iPhone and iPad it is left out of your backups, since it can always be downloaded again. All of this stays on your device. It isn’t sent anywhere and we can’t see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

You choose whether each book keeps your progress. Under Your progress in that book’s Settings, turn off Keep my place on this device and it keeps nothing of your progress, forgetting what it had; Reset progress forgets it at once. Either way your settings stay as they are. To remove a downloaded reading, use Readings on this device in the shelf’s settings.

Network

The books, their pictures and their fonts are inside the app. Each book’s reading is part of the app as well, but kept by the App Store (on iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (on Android) until you first listen to that book, when the app asks the store for it, as it does for the app itself. Apple and Google handle that download under their own privacy policies (Apple’s, Google’s); nothing about you or what you read is sent to us.

Apart from those downloads and from asking the store about your purchases (below), the app makes no network requests. We run no server for it.

Purchases

The in-app purchases (each whole book, and All the books) are made and processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google through Google Play on Android. Each handles your payment and account details under its own privacy policy (Apple’s, Google’s). The app only asks the store which of its purchases your Apple Account or Google account owns, so it can open those books. We never receive your name, email, payment details or account.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

The website

This page, the support page and the app’s page (cyberfugue.github.io) are hosted by GitHub Pages. They load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google, and GitHub, receive the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. The pages themselves set no cookies and run no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
