The Night Land: help

Questions, problems or a purchase that didn't go through: write to cyberfugue@gmail.com.

Say which device you're using and what happened, and you'll get a reply as soon as possible.

What's free, and what the purchase unlocks

The prologue and Chapters I to III are free, with every feature: the painted scenes, the narration, the map, the chapter views, the opening of Take the pen, and the walk within the Last Redoubt. One in-app purchase unlocks the rest of the book, its narration, scenes and map, every turning and ending of Take the pen, and the Night Land beyond the Great Gate to walk. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

Restoring your purchase

On a new iPhone or iPad, or after reinstalling, open the app and tap Restore purchase. It's on the unlock screen, which appears at the end of Chapter III, and under The whole book in Settings. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. You won't be charged again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

The narration

The whole book is read aloud in the LibriVox recording by Mark Nelson, nearly 19 hours, and it's stored inside the app, so it plays without a connection. It keeps playing with the screen locked. While it plays, the text follows the narrator; tap any paragraph to hear it from there. Its loudness, and the loudness of the Land's sounds, are in the Reading settings.

Walking the Land

Tap Walk the Land on the title page, or walk in the bar, to step into the book's places: the Tower of Observation, down by the Great Lift to the Great Causeway, and out at the Great Gate into the Night Land as far as the four Watchers. Drag anywhere to walk; tap a thing to look at it, or someone to speak with them. Outside the Circle, keep to the moss and away from the fire-pits: the eye at the top shows how near the Land is to noticing you.

Your place in the book

The app remembers the chapter and place you reached, and where you stand in the walk, on that device. It doesn't sync between devices. To start afresh, tap Reset progress under Your progress in Settings; to keep nothing between visits, turn off Keep my place on this device there. Your settings stay.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 15 or later. On iPhone Duo the app resizes as you fold and unfold it, and keeps your place.

Privacy

The app collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your reading place and settings stay on your device. The full privacy policy has the details.

The text is the complete 1912 edition of The Night Land, in the public domain. The narration is the LibriVox recording read by Mark Nelson, also in the public domain. The painted scenes, map, notes and Take the pen are original to this edition.
