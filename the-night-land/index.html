A book to step inside

The Night Land is one of the strangest visions in English fiction: a dying earth where the last of humankind shelters in a metal pyramid miles high, ringed by a circle of light that the watching monsters outside cannot cross. One man hears a voice from the dark and goes out alone to find it.

This edition puts that world around the text, so the land you are reading about is there behind the words.

I

A living land behind the text

Every chapter has its own painted scene, drawn from the book's own descriptions: the Redoubt and its Circle, the plain of fire-pits, the gorge, the sea, the House of Silence. Fire flickers, ash drifts, and the land answers what you read.

II

The whole book, read aloud

Nearly nineteen hours of narration, built into the app, with the text following the voice paragraph by paragraph. It plays without a connection, and keeps playing with your screen locked. Tap any paragraph to hear it from there.

III

Look out from the Embrasure

Step out of the text into the chapter's place. Pick out the House of Silence, the Watchers, the Road Where the Silent Ones Walk, and read what the book says about each.

IV

A map of the journey

Hodgson never drew one, so this edition does, from the text. Fog covers whatever the story hasn't reached yet, and the way taken so far is traced in gold.

V

Take the pen

A new telling written for this edition, in which you choose what the narrator does at the story's turning points: thirty passages and eight endings, with the scenes and the map following your path.

About this edition

The text is the complete 1912 edition, in the public domain. The narration is the LibriVox recording read by Mark Nelson, also in the public domain. The painted scenes, the map, the notes on the Land and Take the pen are original to this edition.

The app collects no data. It works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and on iPhone Duo it resizes as you fold and unfold, keeping your place.
