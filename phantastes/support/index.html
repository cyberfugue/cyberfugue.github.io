Phantastes: help

Questions or problems: write to cyberfugue@gmail.com.

Say which device you’re using and what happened, and you’ll get a reply as soon as possible.

What’s free, and what the purchase opens

The mottoes and Chapter I are free, with everything the edition does: its plates alive, both readings word by word, the painted wood and its sound, Wander in the wood, the drawers of the old secretary, and all twenty-five of John Bell’s plates to look through. One in-app purchase, The whole book ($3.99, shown on the title page in your own currency), opens the rest: Chapters II to XXV, both readings for all of them, every place of the painted Fairy Land, the whole chart, and the moments (the marble lady, the globe, the hall of Phantasy, the cottage of four doors). There is no subscription and there are no ads.

Restoring your purchase

On a new phone or tablet, or after reinstalling, open the app and tap Restore purchase. It’s on the purchase’s screen, which opens from Buy the whole book on the title page and appears where the free part ends (at the end of Chapter I), and under The whole book in Settings. Use the same Apple Account (iPhone and iPad) or Google account (Android) you bought with. You won’t be charged again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone and iPad, ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with your Apple Account. On Android, go to your Google Play order history and choose the purchase.

The readers

Tap listen in the bar, or Begin, read aloud on the title page. Two LibriVox readings are inside the app, so they play without a connection, with the silent switch on and with the screen locked: Brad Powers and Thomas A. Copeland. Choose between them under Settings → The reader’s voice; the reading carries on from the same place. The word being read is lit; turn that off with Light each word as it is read. Tap any paragraph to hear it from there. The dock beneath the page goes back and on fifteen seconds, changes the speed, sets a sleep timer, and lets the page follow the voice or not; the lock screen and headphones can pause and play.

Sound

The sound of Fairy Land, and its music, are made as you read. They are on until you turn them off: check sound in the bar (it reads “sound on” or “sound off”), and the levels of the places and the music in Settings. Headphones are best.

The plates, the Shadow and the moments

The plates come quietly alive when they reach the middle of the screen; tap one to see it full size, and swipe between them. From the eighth chapter a Shadow follows the page, as it follows Anodos; where it falls, the plates turn grey. Both can be turned off under Settings → Reading aids. Where the book gives you something to do (the drawers of the old secretary, the songs, the globe, the four doors), an offer appears in the margin of the text.

Your place

The app remembers your place and your settings on that device. Continue on the title page takes you back to it; Begin always starts at the beginning of the book; home in the bar returns to the title page from anywhere. Your place doesn’t sync between devices. To start afresh, tap Reset progress under Your progress in Settings; to keep nothing between visits, turn off Keep my place on this device there. Your settings stay.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with a recent iOS, or an Android phone or tablet with Android 7 or later. Fairy Land is painted live, so a recent device gives the smoothest picture.

Privacy

The app collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The full privacy policy has the details.

Phantastes: A Faerie Romance for Men and Women, by George MacDonald (1858), is in the public domain, as are John Bell’s plates for the edition of 1894 and the LibriVox readings by Brad Powers and Thomas A. Copeland. The painted Fairy Land, the sound and music, the map and the moments were made for this edition.
