The Time Machine · iPhone and iPad

Privacy policy

Effective 17 October 2026

In short: the app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you do in it is sent to us.

What stays on your device

To let you carry on where you left off, the app saves a few things on your iPhone or iPad: your place in the journey and in the book, the moments of the journey you have reached (with what was in your pockets and your notebook), your settings (sound levels, text size, captions, the people), and whether you have unlocked the whole edition. This stays on your device. It isn't sent anywhere and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase is made and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment and account details under its own privacy policy. The app only asks the App Store whether this device's Apple Account owns the purchase, so it can open the rest of the edition. We never receive your name, email, payment details or Apple Account.

Network

The book, its narration, the people and the fonts are all inside the app, so it plays without a connection. Apart from the App Store purchase checks above, the app makes no network requests. The one link out, to librivox.org in About this edition, opens in Safari only if you tap it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

The website

This page, the support page and the app’s page (cyberfugue.github.io) are hosted by GitHub Pages. They load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google, and GitHub, receive the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. The pages themselves set no cookies and run no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
