The Time Machine: help

Questions, problems or a purchase that didn't go through: write to cyberfugue@gmail.com.

Say which device you're using and what happened, and you'll get a reply as soon as possible.

What's free, and what the purchase unlocks

The first three chapters are free: Read the book and Listen to the book for Chapters I to III, and the journey from the drawing-room at Richmond to the machine in the laboratory. One in-app purchase, the whole edition, unlocks all the rest:

the rest of the book, read and heard; the voyage itself and the levers in your hands, the Golden Age, Weena, the Underworld, the Palace of Green Porcelain, the burning forest, the trap of the White Sphinx, the last beach and the way home; Take the machine, the open voyage to any year; and every moment of the journey to return to.

There is no subscription and there are no ads.

Restoring your purchase

On a new phone or tablet, or after reinstalling, open the app and tap Restore purchase. It's on the purchase's screen, which appears where the free part ends, and in the Settings. Use the same Apple Account (iPhone and iPad) or Google account (Android) you bought with. You won't be charged again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone and iPad, ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with your Apple Account. On Android, go to your Google Play order history and choose the purchase.

The machine

Sit in the saddle and drag the starting lever over; the further it goes, the faster you travel, from hours a second to ten million years. The stopping lever brakes, and past rest takes you back. Go too fast for too long and the quartz overheats: the governor trips and holds the levers until it cools. If the machine is hard to bring down on a date, turn on Help at the levers in the Settings.

Sound and the voice

Every sound is made live by the app; turn on Hear it on the title page. Headphones are best. The book is read aloud in the LibriVox recording by Cliff Stone, which is stored inside the app, so it plays without a connection, and goes on with the screen locked; the lock screen and headphones can pause and play it, and go back or on fifteen seconds. The voice, the places and the music each have their own loudness in the Settings, and Captions for sounds writes them on the screen.

Your place

The app remembers your place in the journey and the book, and every moment of the journey you have reached, on that device. It doesn't sync between devices. To start afresh, tap Reset progress under Your progress in Settings (or in About this edition on the title page); to keep nothing between visits, turn off Keep my place on this device there. Your settings stay.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 15 or later, or an Android phone or tablet with Android 7 or later. The world is drawn live in 3D, so a recent device gives the smoothest picture. If it runs slowly, People: Drawn in the Settings is lighter than the realistic people.

Privacy

The app collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your place and settings stay on your device. The full privacy policy has the details.

The text is the 1895 Heinemann edition of The Time Machine, in the public domain. The narration is the LibriVox recording read by Cliff Stone, also in the public domain. The realistic people are from Microsoft's Rocketbox library (MIT licence). Everything else seen and heard was made for this edition.
