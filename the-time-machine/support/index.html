The Time Machine: help

Questions, problems or a purchase that didn't go through: write to cyberfugue@gmail.com.

Say which device you're using and what happened, and you'll get a reply as soon as possible.

What's free, and what the purchases unlock

The first three chapters are free: Read the book and Listen to the book for Chapters I to III, and the journey from the drawing-room at Richmond to the machine in the laboratory. Two in-app purchases unlock the rest, each on its own:

The whole book: the rest of the book, read and heard.

The whole journey: the voyage itself and the levers in your hands, the Golden Age, Weena, the Underworld, the Palace of Green Porcelain, the burning forest, the trap of the White Sphinx, the last beach and the way home; Take the machine, the open voyage to any year; and every moment of the journey to return to.

There is no subscription and there are no ads.

Restoring your purchases

On a new iPhone or iPad, or after reinstalling, open the app and tap Restore purchase. It's on each purchase's screen, which appears where its free part ends, and in the Settings. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. You won't be charged again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

The machine

Sit in the saddle and press the starting lever over (drag it, or use W and S on a keyboard); the further it goes, the faster you travel, from hours a second to ten million years. The stopping lever brakes, and past rest takes you back. Go too fast for too long and the quartz overheats: the governor trips and holds the levers until it cools. If the machine is hard to bring down on a date, turn on Help at the levers in the Settings.

Sound and the voice

Every sound is made live by the app; turn on Hear it on the title page. Headphones are best. The book is read aloud in the LibriVox recording by Cliff Stone, which is stored inside the app, so it plays without a connection. The voice, the places and the music each have their own loudness in the Settings, and Captions for sounds writes them on the screen.

Your place

The app remembers your place in the journey and the book, and every moment of the journey you have reached, on that device. It doesn't sync between devices.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 15 or later. The world is drawn live in 3D, so a recent device gives the smoothest picture. If it runs slowly, People: Drawn in the Settings is lighter than the realistic people.

Privacy

The app collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your place and settings stay on your device. The full privacy policy has the details.

The text is the 1895 Heinemann edition of The Time Machine, in the public domain. The narration is the LibriVox recording read by Cliff Stone, also in the public domain. The realistic people are from Microsoft's Rocketbox library (MIT licence). Everything else seen and heard was made for this edition.
