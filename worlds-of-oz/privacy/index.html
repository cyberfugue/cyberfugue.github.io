Worlds of Oz · iPhone, iPad and Android

Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

In short: the app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone.

What stays on your device

Each book keeps a few things on your phone or tablet so that you can carry on: your place in it, the chapters you have reached, the moments you have done, and your settings for it (text size, sound and music, who reads, how Oz is shown). Your room keeps the keepsakes you have found, where you have set them, and the colour of its walls. The library keeps the way into Oz you chose, which book you last opened, and what the store said you own. The Wizard’s workshop keeps its notebook, the experiments you have found there. Who’s Who remembers whether you want what happens in unread books shown or covered. Each book’s reading, once downloaded, is kept too, until you remove it. All of this stays on your device; it isn’t sent anywhere and we can’t see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Under Your progress in a book’s Settings, turn off Keep my place on this device and it keeps nothing of your progress; Reset progress forgets it at once. Empty the room, in the room’s Settings, forgets your keepsakes; Clear the notebook, in the workshop’s Settings, forgets its experiments.

Network

The books, their pictures and fonts are inside the app. Each book’s reading is kept by the App Store (on iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (on Android) until you first listen to that book, when the app asks the store for it. Apple and Google handle that download under their own privacy policies (Apple’s, Google’s). Apart from those downloads and asking the store about your purchases, the app makes no network requests. We run no server for it.

Purchases

The in-app purchases are made and processed by Apple or Google, under their own privacy policies. The app only asks the store which of its purchases your account owns, so it can open those books. We never receive your name, email, payment details or account.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

The website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and load fonts from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google receive the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. The pages set no cookies and run no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
