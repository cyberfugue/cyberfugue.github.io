Support

Write to cyberfugue@gmail.com with your device and what happened, and we’ll help.

Realms of Oz was called Worlds of Oz. Only the name changed: your books, purchases, places, keepsakes and room are all where you left them, and Restore purchase finds what you bought under either name.

The library and the map of Oz

The library opens on the painted Land of Oz, with each book a numbered pin in its own country and the books in their covers beside it. Tap a pin to bring its book forward, then Read book. Tap a country’s name for its books, or the Emerald City for your room.

Map in the library’s bar, or the Magic Picture on the wall of the Wizard’s workshop, opens the map of Oz full screen. Drag to move about; pinch, or double-tap, to come closer, and double-tap again to go back out. Tap a pin for its book’s card, with Read book and Close. The red pin at the head of the road of yellow brick opens Stories of Oz. Explore Oz or The library in the bar brings you back to the library.

Reading and listening

Begin opens the book at its start; Continue opens it where you left off on this device. Tap listen in the bar to hear the book read aloud from where you are, with each word lit as it is spoken; tap any paragraph to hear it from there. Choose the reader in Settings. Each book’s reading is downloaded the first time you listen, and then plays without a connection; remove it under Readings on this device in the library’s Settings.

How Oz is shown

Choose Storybook, Walk into Oz or Paper theatre on the book’s title page, with the bar’s look button, or in Settings. Drag the walk or the theatre to look about. To read with no picture beside the text, turn off Show Oz beside the text in Settings.

Keepsakes and your room

A keepsake sparkles in the text and in Oz where Baum tells of it; tap it to take it. Some are earned in a moment (a ✦ in the text). In your room, tap a keepsake in the satchel and then a place that glows, or drag it there; tap one in the room to move it or put it back. Swipe the room sideways to its alcoves, one for each book of Oz; a book's alcove opens when you find the first of its keepsakes. Resetting a book’s progress never takes your keepsakes; only Empty the room, in the room’s Settings, does.

The Wizard’s workshop

Beside your room (the workshop button in the room’s bar) is the Wizard’s workshop, where the magic things you have found in the books can be tried on whatever stands on the bench: the Powder of Life on the saw-horse or the sofa, the oil-can on the Tin Woodman, the Golden Cap on its own. Tap a magic thing to take it in hand, then tap what you would try it on, or tap Use it. Some need their words said first, in order, as Baum gives them. Each experiment found is written in the Wizard’s notebook. Nothing is used up there; the books still count the Golden Cap’s calls and the wishing pills.

Who’s Who in Oz

Also beside your room (who’s who in the room’s bar) is an encyclopedia of the people of Oz and a bestiary of its creatures. Tap a portrait to open a profile: where they first appear (tap Read it to go there), their story, nature and powers, their friends and enemies, how much they are in each of the fourteen books, and what becomes of them book by book. What happens in a book you haven’t begun is covered: tap Uncover, or choose Show everything in its Settings.

Stories of Oz

Tap Play in the library’s bar or your room’s, the painted box on the hearth-rug in your room, or the red pin at the head of the road of yellow brick on the map of Oz; home takes you back the way you came. When you begin, choose how to go into Oz: The Tellings, where you play each book’s own hero, or The Traveller, a child of your own from the next farm in Kansas, who walks beside every story. Drag anywhere to walk; tap a person to talk to them, then tap what you would say; touch and hold to look at something. Your game is saved as you go, and Save / Load in the ☰ menu keeps three games of your own and can download a backup of them. A keepsake found in the game is the same keepsake as in the book, and waits for you in your room. A game played on an earlier version of the app carries on where you left it.

On iPhone Duo, opening the phone opens Ozma’s Magic Picture: the view widens to show more of Oz on the inner display, and your game carries on where it was. Folding the phone brings the nearer view back.

Purchases

Chapter I of each book is free. The whole book opens the rest of a book, and its part of Stories of Oz; All the books of Oz opens every book and all of Stories of Oz, including every one added later. In the game, the cyclone, Kansas and the Munchkins’ meadow are free. On a new device, use Restore purchase in the book’s Settings or the library’s, signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

Your progress

Under Your progress in a book’s Settings, Keep my place on this device chooses whether the book remembers where you are, and Reset progress forgets it.
