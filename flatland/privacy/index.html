Flatland · iPhone and iPad

Privacy policy

Effective 17 October 2026

In short: the app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you do in it is sent to us.

What stays on your device

So that you can carry on where you left off, the app keeps a few things on your iPhone or iPad: your place in the reading, your settings (sound, voice and ambient levels, text size, who reads, the figures), and whether you have bought the whole book. This stays on your device. It isn’t sent anywhere and we can’t see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

You choose whether it is kept. Under Your progress in Settings, turn off Keep my place on this device and the app keeps nothing of your progress, forgetting what it had; Reset progress forgets it at once. Either way your settings stay as they are.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase is made and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment and account details under its own privacy policy. The app only asks the App Store whether this device’s Apple Account owns the purchase, so that it can open the rest of the book. We never receive your name, email, payment details or Apple Account.

Network

The book, its figures and Ruth Golding’s recording are all inside the app, so it works without a connection. The voice cast uses your device’s own voices. Apart from the App Store purchase checks above, the app makes no network requests. The one link out, to the recording’s page at archive.org, opens in Safari only if you tap it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

The website

This page, the support page and the app’s page (cyberfugue.github.io) are hosted by GitHub Pages. They load a font from Google Fonts, so Google, and GitHub, receive the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. The pages themselves set no cookies and run no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
