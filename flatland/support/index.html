Flatland: help

Questions, problems or a purchase that didn’t go through: write to cyberfugue@gmail.com.

Say which device you’re using and what happened, and you’ll get a reply as soon as possible.

What’s free, and what the purchase unlocks

The first three sections of the book are free, read and heard: §1 Of the Nature of Flatland, §2 Of the Climate and Houses in Flatland, and §3 Concerning the Inhabitants of Flatland. So are the four worlds, Lineland, Flatland, Spaceland and Hyperland, to explore as long as you like.

The whole book ($3.99, once) unlocks the rest: all twenty-two sections and the editor’s preface, in the reader and read aloud. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

Restoring your purchase

On a new iPhone or iPad, or after reinstalling, open the app and tap Restore purchase. It’s in Settings (⚙ in the bar at the top), and on the offer that appears when you reach §4. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. You won’t be charged again.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Sound and the voice

The sound is on until you turn it off: check Sound in the bar (it reads “sound on” or “sound off”), and the Voice and Ambient levels in Settings. Headphones are best. The book is read aloud by Ruth Golding (LibriVox), stored inside the app, so it plays without a connection, with the silent switch on and with the screen locked; the lock screen and headphones can pause and play. The editor’s preface isn’t in her recording, so a cast of your device’s own voices reads it; you can choose the voice cast for the whole book in Settings.

The worlds

Tap 1D to 4D to move between Lineland, Flatland, Spaceland and Hyperland. Drag to walk or to turn the view; the slider lowers the Sphere through the plane, or slides the slice through the fourth dimension. While the book is read aloud, the stage follows the story on its own (Watch), or leaves the controls to you (Explore).

Your place

The app remembers your place in the reading and your settings on that device. Continue on the title page takes you back to it; Begin always starts at the beginning of the book; Home (⌂) returns to the title page from anywhere. Your place doesn’t sync between devices.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 15 or later. The worlds are drawn live in 3D, so a recent device gives the smoothest picture.

Privacy

The app collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your place and settings stay on your device. The full privacy policy has the details.

Flatland: A Romance of Many Dimensions, by Edwin A. Abbott (second edition, 1884), is in the public domain, as are his figures and Ruth Golding’s LibriVox recording. The four worlds, the redrawn figures and the sound were made for this edition.
