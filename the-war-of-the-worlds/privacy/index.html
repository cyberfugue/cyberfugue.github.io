The War of the Worlds · iPhone, iPad and Android

Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

In short: the app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone.

What stays on your device

So that you can carry on where you left off, the app keeps a few things on your phone or tablet: your place in the book, how far you have read in each chapter, which plates you have seen, your settings (text size, sound and music levels, who reads, the drawn world and the reading aids), and whether you have bought the whole book. This stays on your device. It isn’t sent anywhere and we can’t see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

You choose whether your progress is kept. Under Your progress in Settings, turn off Keep my place on this device and the app keeps nothing of your progress, forgetting what it had; Reset progress forgets it at once. Either way your settings stay as they are.

Network

The book, its plates, its fonts and both readings are all inside the app, so it works without a connection. Apart from asking the App Store or Google Play about the purchase (below), it makes no network requests, and it has no links out.

Purchases

The one in-app purchase is made and processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google through Google Play on Android. Each handles your payment and account details under its own privacy policy (Apple’s, Google’s). The app only asks the store whether your Apple Account or Google account owns the purchase, so it can open the rest of the book. We never receive your name, email, payment details or account.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

The website

This page, the support page and the app’s page (cyberfugue.github.io) are hosted by GitHub Pages. They load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google, and GitHub, receive the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address. The pages themselves set no cookies and run no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
